# Separate nighttime fitting collection addendum

This addendum authorizes only a separate observation collection and its quality checks. The existing daytime/fresh-evaluation v2 plan and all earlier evaluation identities remain unchanged. No fitting or deployment criterion is changed here.

The root-authorized extension targets six new qualifying nighttime dates for each of Greater London and Sioux Falls in 2021–2022. The immutable offline inventory excludes all previous 200 attempted pilot-orbits and their whole pilot-dates, plus every previously inspected pilot-date in the root registry. Alternate tiles from those orbits cannot reintroduce their native physical observations. A geographic cell observed on a new date remains a new observation. No 2023, 2024 or 2025 thermal labels enter this extension.

Metadata selection requires a consistent footprint with positive pilot overlap and actual solar elevation at most −6° at the centre and all corners. Latest processing build/iteration is selected per scene/tile before choosing the greatest overlap per orbit. Within each month and three-hour solar bin, at most four acquisition ranks from the fixed SHA ordering are retained. Candidates alternate between pilots. Within a pilot, one candidate is taken from each nonempty calendar-quarter/year stratum before a second candidate from that stratum; the stratum order is quarter then year, alternating 2021 and 2022 within each quarter. Within a stratum the order is rank-within-month/hour, month, solar bin and fixed SHA. Thermal values, residuals and accuracy outcomes never affect ordering.

The offline inventory found 188 remaining London dates and 183 Sioux dates, but no positive matching GEO flags cached for those opportunities. Before thermal reads, each selected candidate still requires complete anonymous GEO discovery, its latest matching processing build and a positively decoded Good/Best geolocation flag. Missing, Suspect, Poor and conflicting flags fail. The complete official obstruction list excludes confirmed or ambiguous obstruction; absence is not claimed as proof of unobstructed pixels.

The reviewed V002 decoder remains unchanged: native QA, source uncertainty and view-angle gates, 350 m cloud/nodata exclusion, at least 90% valid area, fixed 100 m grid, full source hashes and revision identities. A separate native fitting support mask clears permanent held-out blocks, 1 km buffers and a conservative native-footprint margin. A source date qualifies for collection counting with at least 200 fitting-safe cells across two nonreserved 10 km blocks with at least 50 cells each. Station/weather/optical availability and final row admission remain separate; these thresholds do not guarantee model improvement.

The new campaign has independent hard bounds of 100 candidates and 1 GiB additional network transfer, including public metadata, GEO companions, failed bundles and conservatively reserved interrupted transfers. Cached assets may be reused with identity/hash checks. Process limits are two CPUs and 2 GiB RAM, one acquisition worker at a time; batches resume from immutable plan-bound state. The processing-time ceiling is 20 minutes per pilot. As in the reviewed v2 runner, interrupted in-flight elapsed time is not charged to this soft ceiling; the hard attempt and byte reservations remain durable. Results commit before clearing recoverable in-flight state. No production model or earlier frozen adapter is modified.

New source rows preserve exact observation times, native geometry, source hash, valid-area fraction, geolocation/cloud proofs and native fitting-support status. Deterministic support-only sampling supplies disjoint immutable feature batches; source-screened is not synonymous with training-eligible.
