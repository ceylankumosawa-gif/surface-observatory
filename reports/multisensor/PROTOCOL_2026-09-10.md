## Fixed fine-data and calibration comparison

**F** is the same 40-input histogram-gradient-boosting specification as E, fitted once to all E fitting rows plus new admitted fine observations. Preserve model capacity, seed, target LST minus air temperature and balanced weighting algorithm. Sensor ID, site identity and coordinates are not added as predictors.

**G** adds a small residual correction learned only from out-of-fold 2021–2022 F predictions. There are three fixed folds: `((year − 2021) × 12 + month − 1) modulo 3`. Each holds complete global UTC year-months across all pilots and sensors. No 2023, 2024 or 2025 label enters this correction fit.

Fit weighted Ridge with alpha 20 to observed LST minus out-of-fold prediction. Numeric predictors are predicted surface–air offset, air temperature, solar elevation, cloud fraction, wind, humidity and `log1p(snow_water_equivalent_m / 0.001)`. Add one-hot broad climate groups A–E and day/night phase. Standardize numeric fields with fitting weights. Scale Ridge weights by the effective independent global UTC-date count, `1 / sum(date_weight²)`, rather than pixel count; duplicating many pixels must not weaken regularization.

Apply half the fitted residual correction, clipped to ±3°C, only for climate-group/phase combinations with at least six distinct UTC dates across three year-months and two out-of-fold groups. Other combinations retain F with an explicit unsupported-calibration status. This is a conservative correction bound, **not an error guarantee**. Keep alpha, shrinkage, support thresholds and clipping fixed. No parameter search, residual-based source selection or repeated refit is allowed after evaluation.

Compare E, F and G on exactly the same previous evaluation rows and weights, retaining A/v1/air-only references where applicable. Save and hash the final model and correction before evaluation. Report new 2023 fine-source checks separately from old rows. Only after model freeze run the same previously inspected 2024 reference check. Preserve the 2025 blind data boundary.

Report balanced and ordinary pixel MAE, signed bias, weighted and raw >5/>7°C error shares, spatial contrast, per-source results, day/night and hot/cold support. Date counts must distinguish global UTC dates from pilot-date combinations. Show regressions as well as gains. Repeated 2023/2024 reference tests are exploratory evidence, not fresh blind confirmation. A successful engineering download or lower pooled average does not establish hourly, all-weather or global 100 m accuracy.
