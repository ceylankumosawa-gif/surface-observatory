# Retrospective weather-baseline comparison

This protocol is fixed before new ERA5-Land temperature values or experiment scores are inspected. The question is whether a modeled land-surface baseline and soil/snow state improve the shared research learner without worsening large errors or destroying local thermal contrast. Numerical work runs only on Hetzner. No website change, model promotion, new satellite labels, or reserved 2025 label access is authorized by this experiment.

## Inputs and source support

Reuse the immutable, verified 44,316 fitting observations from 2021–22 in `/opt/lst-pilot/runs/shared_model_remedies_20260910_v1/fit_frame.parquet`. Bind this input, source code, prior F/Extra Trees references and this protocol by hashes in the new run. Never overwrite previous experiment outputs.

Acquire only official ERA5-Land hourly skin temperature, 2 m air temperature, topsoil temperature (0–7 cm), topsoil volumetric moisture (0–7 cm) and snow water equivalent. Record source URLs, metadata/variable identifiers, units, object hashes, native coordinates and selected valid time. Convert Kelvin temperatures once to Celsius. Require snow water equivalent (`sd`, GRIB parameter 141, metres of water equivalent); physical snow depth `sde` is not interchangeable. A standard CDS subset of the same ERA5-Land variable is acceptable if the ARCO store lacks it; record that delivery path and match the grid, release and valid time. Do not silently substitute ERA5, a forecast product, soil temperature for skin temperature, or a nearby land cell.

Select the nearest native 0.1-degree ERA5-Land cell and the exact UTC floor hour for each row. Use the same cell/time for all new fields. Record weather age, including its maximum of less than 60 minutes. No next-hour interpolation. This is a retrospective experiment: the reanalysis and its assimilated atmospheric forcing need not have been available operationally at the target time. Coarse physical skin temperature is a model estimate at roughly 9 km support, not an observed or validated 100 m thermal label.

Initially bound ERA5-Land extraction to 1 GiB, 2,000 requests and 30 minutes per staged cohort; inspect any limit before changing it. ICON-DREAM retrieval is a separate, bounded diagnostic using verified byte ranges. There is no need to retain entire global months or enlarge storage for the planned pilot extracts.

Primary learned comparisons require complete, finite new physical inputs on all 44,316 fitting rows. If any are missing or metadata cannot establish the intended variable, stop those arms and report the coverage problem. Never silently drop rows, fill missing cells with zero or compare a reduced cohort with the original reference. Apply the same complete-cohort rule to each existing evaluation cohort; any unsupported observations must be counted and disclosed before a separately labeled matched-subset diagnostic can be considered.

## Seven fixed arms

Let `Y` be observed fine-resolution LST, `A` the existing unchanged `air_temperature_c`, `S` ERA5-Land skin temperature and `T` ERA5-Land 2 m air temperature. Define `B = A + (S − T)`. Keep the existing station/background-air adjustment exactly as stored; do not replace it with raw station readings or apply a second elevation correction.

1. `F`: existing shared research model, reproduced from saved same-fold OOF and full-fit references.
2. `blend_et25`: prior fixed `0.75 F + 0.25 Extra Trees` research reference, using matching fold components.
3. `raw_land_skin`: `S`, no fitted correction.
4. `station_adjusted_land`: `B`, no fitted correction.
5. `land_features`: the shared F architecture fits `Y − A` with the 40 existing predictors and four declared physical inputs; prediction `A + f(X)`.
6. `land_residual`: the identical architecture and predictors fit `Y − B`; prediction `B + g(X)`.
7. `land_blend25`: globally fixed `0.75 F + 0.25 land_residual`. No fitted mixing coefficient or climate/season/pilot routing.

The four additional predictors are `land_skin_minus_air_c = S − T`, `land_soil_minus_air_c = topsoil_temperature − T`, `land_snow_water_equivalent_m` and `land_soil_moisture_m3_m3`. Both learned land arms have identical feature lists, observations, weights and capacity. Preserve `A` as a predictor and construct the residual target explicitly instead of overwriting the air column. No coordinates, pilot IDs, sensor IDs or evaluation-derived corrections become predictors.

The two newly fitted estimators use F's fixed HGB configuration: squared-error loss, 150 iterations, learning rate .08, 15 leaves, maximum depth 6, minimum leaf size 30, L2 penalty 1, no early stopping, seed 2708. Reuse F's numeric and categorical-climate preprocessing with four native threads at most.

## Cross-validation and selection

Use the same three global calendar-month folds: `((UTC year − 2021) * 12 + UTC month − 1) % 3`. Exclude each held month across all pilots, and stitch exactly one prediction for each fitting row. Recompute the unchanged balancing weights inside each training fold: equal pilot/phase, then date, acquisition, surface group and pixel contributions, normalized to sum one and multiplied by the fitting row count. No full-data component enters OOF predictions or a blend.

Only `land_features`, `land_residual` and `land_blend25` are eligible new remedies. The other arms are diagnostic references. To qualify relative to F on the same stitched OOF rows:

- Overall balanced MAE improves by at least 0.10°C.
- Ordinary-observation MAE worsens by no more than 0.10°C.
- Every supported pilot/phase's balanced MAE worsens by no more than 0.20°C.
- Overall and every supported pilot/phase's weighted frequency of absolute errors above 5°C and above 7°C each worsen by no more than 0.02 (two percentage points).
- Overall acquisition-centered contrast error worsens by no more than 0.10°C.

Supported pilot/phase means at least six distinct UTC dates. Rank qualifying remedies by balanced MAE; candidates within 0.05°C of the best use this fixed preference order: `land_features`, `land_residual`, `land_blend25`. Preserve a no-qualifying-candidate result. Do not weaken any gate after seeing scores. Freeze OOF evidence and the decision before decoding evaluation labels; subsequently fit all fixed learned arms on all admitted fitting observations for evaluation.

## Held-out checks, weather diagnosis and interpretation

After selection, score all arms on the same old, new and legacy evaluation cohorts from the previous comparison. These contain held-out groups and 2021–24 observations, including repeatedly examined 2023/24 checks. They may reject the selected remedy but may not retrospectively choose or tune it. Report balanced and ordinary MAE, signed bias, >5°C and >7°C rates and counts, acquisition-centered contrast error, cohort size, date support, and breakdowns by pilot/phase, source, season and hot/cold observed conditions. Keep London early-spring acquisitions and Sioux Falls January 7, 2023 nighttime failures visible. Preserve reserved 2025 thermal labels unopened.

ICON-DREAM cross-checks the existing Sioux Falls January 7, 2023 11:04:36 UTC, London February 5, 2023 14:19:13 UTC and London April 17, 2023 09:47:42 UTC events. Request 2 m temperature and total cloud cover first; radiation or low-level temperature context is optional if inexpensive. Record exact domain, grid/resolution, selected point, valid time and field semantics. Keep floor-hour and adjacent-hour diagnostics distinct. ICON-DREAM is a second reanalysis, not independent surface-temperature truth, and cannot by itself establish whether a satellite cloud mask or ERA5 is wrong.

An independent audit must verify extraction metadata/unit/time consistency, identical sample identities, folds, targets, weights, prediction reconstruction, selection and reported metrics. Keep a reproducible executed notebook and a source-backed comparison report. Report actual time/storage use and all material limitations. No coarse-grid smoothing or added noise counts as improved fine-resolution accuracy.
